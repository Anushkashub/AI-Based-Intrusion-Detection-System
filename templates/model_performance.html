Academic & Viva Demonstration Notice

Important distinction for project presentation and viva examination:

1. Model Benchmark Evaluation (Offline)

The 96.39% accuracy and associated metrics represent the rigorous offline evaluation of the pre-trained Random Forest model on the test partition of the full benchmark CIC-IDS2017 dataset.

2. Live Traffic Inference (Online)

When you upload network captures in Traffic Analysis, the frozen model performs live forward-pass inference. The Dashboard and Threat views display the actual classifications generated from your specific uploaded file.
